# Fortune Cookie for the Liquidated Trader

One line: a fortune cookie for a trader who has just been liquidated. It opens in the classic oracular voice — great wealth, where you least expect it — and the punchline turns the fortune back on the reader: the wealth is sitting in the wallet he just emptied.

> You will find great wealth where you least expect it: in the very wallet you just emptied.
